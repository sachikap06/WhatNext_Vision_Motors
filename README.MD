# WhatNext Vision Motors: Shaping the Future of Mobility with Innovation and Excellence

## Project Overview

WhatNext Vision Motors is a Salesforce-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) project developed to simplify and automate automobile business operations. It provides a centralized platform for managing vehicle customers, vehicles, dealers, vehicle orders, test drives, and service requests.

The project uses Salesforce Flows, Apex Triggers, Apex Classes, Batch Apex, and Scheduled Apex to automate business processes, validate vehicle stock, process pending orders, and send test-drive reminders. Reports and dashboards provide a clear overview of vehicle inventory and related business information.

## Objectives

- Manage vehicle customers and their information.
- Maintain vehicle inventory, pricing, and stock availability.
- Manage dealer information and automate dealer assignment.
- Create and track vehicle orders.
- Schedule test drives and send automated reminders.
- Validate stock availability and update inventory automatically.
- Process pending vehicle orders using Batch Apex and Scheduled Apex.
- Generate reports and dashboards for inventory monitoring.

## Technologies Used

- **Platform:** Salesforce Lightning Experience
- **Programming Language:** Apex
- **Automation:** Salesforce Record-Triggered Flows
- **Asynchronous Processing:** Batch Apex and Scheduled Apex
- **Data Management:** Salesforce Custom Objects and Fields
- **Reporting:** Salesforce Reports and Dashboards

## Key Features

1. **Customer Management** – Maintains customer details and vehicle preferences.
2. **Vehicle Management** – Tracks vehicle models, prices, status, and stock quantity.
3. **Dealer Management** – Maintains dealer information and supports automated dealer assignment.
4. **Vehicle Order Management** – Manages orders and checks stock availability.
5. **Test Drive Management** – Organizes test-drive records and automated email reminders.
6. **Service Request Management** – Maintains customer vehicle service requests.
7. **Apex Trigger and Handler** – Validates stock and updates inventory for confirmed orders.
8. **Batch Apex** – Processes eligible pending vehicle orders when stock is available.
9. **Scheduled Apex** – Schedules batch processing automatically.
10. **Reports and Dashboards** – Provides an overview of vehicle inventory and availability.

## Testing

The project features were tested using Salesforce records and automation tools. Testing covered dealer assignment, test-drive reminder emails, stock validation, inventory updates, and batch processing of pending orders.
